Privacy policy

How Auxbow handles your data.

What this site collects, why, who else handles it, and what you can ask for. Last updated 1 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Auxbow is the controller of the personal data collected through auxbow.io. For anything about your data, email hello@auxbow.io.

What the site collects

When you send the enquiry form

Your name, work email, company, what you lead, and the problem in your words. Only the problem is required. The form is delivered by Formspree, which receives what you type and standard details of the request, such as your IP address, so it can deliver the message and filter spam.

If the form can't send, the site opens your email app with the message filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

When you email Auxbow

Your name, email address and whatever you write.

Analytics, only if you accept

If you accept cookies, Google Analytics records how the site is used: the pages you view, how you arrived (for example from a search engine or a link), your device and browser type, your approximate location to city level, and whether you sent the enquiry form, but not what you wrote in it. Google Analytics doesn't store your IP address.

Before you choose, or if you decline, no analytics cookies are set. The page still sends Google a basic signal with no identifier, such as that a page was viewed and that you declined, which Google uses to estimate overall traffic.

What the site keeps in your browser

  • _ga and _ga_5JTL8716YV: Google Analytics cookies, set only if you accept. They tell one visit from another and expire after 2 years.
  • auxbow-consent: your cookie choice, so the site doesn't ask again. Kept in your browser's storage until you clear it.
  • auxbow-route: whether you chose the marketing or the media view, so the page opens on it next time. Kept in your browser's storage until you clear it.

The last two never leave your browser.

When the site loads

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its logs to keep the service secure. Auxbow doesn't receive these logs.

Why Auxbow uses it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss working together. The legal basis is Auxbow's legitimate interest in answering business enquiries, or steps you ask for before a contract.
  • To understand how people find and use the site, and to improve it. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from Cookie settings at the foot of the page.
  • To remember your cookie choice and your view. The legal basis is Auxbow's legitimate interest in keeping the choices you made.

Auxbow doesn't sell your data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) delivers form submissions and keeps a copy in Auxbow's account.
  • Google runs Google Analytics, only if you accept. Google processes the data for Auxbow and, under its terms, may also use it to maintain and improve its own services.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the site.
  • Auxbow's email provider holds enquiries and replies.

Auxbow shares data otherwise only where the law requires it, or with professional advisers bound by confidentiality.

Transfers outside the UK

Formspree, Google and GitHub may process data in the United States. These transfers are protected by the UK–US data bridge or by the UK's approved data transfer clauses.

How long it is kept

  • Enquiries and the emails that follow: while the conversation is active, then up to 2 years after the last contact. If you become a client, records are kept for as long as the contract and the law require, usually 6 years.
  • Google Analytics: detailed data for 2 months, after which only aggregated reports remain. The cookies expire after 2 years.
  • Hosting logs: kept by GitHub under its own policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask Auxbow to:

  • give you a copy of your data
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict or object to how it is used
  • send it to you or another organisation in a portable format

You can withdraw consent to analytics at any time from Cookie settings, without affecting anything before. To make a request, email hello@auxbow.io. Auxbow replies within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, please tell Auxbow first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children
